# Review Summary & Publish Your Job

Review all details before making your listing public to workers.

1. Inspect the **Listing Preview** and **Summary** card displaying your chosen service, engagement type, schedule, location, and rate.
2. Tap the checkbox to review and agree to the SerbiSure **Terms & Conditions**.
3. When everything is verified, tap the primary **Post Listing** button.
4. You will see an animated success confirmation! Your job is now live on the marketplace for verified kasambahay workers to view and apply for.
