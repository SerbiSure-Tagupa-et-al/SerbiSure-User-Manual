# Add Household Instructions & Special Notes

Give workers a clear picture of your specific household expectations.

1. SerbiSure automatically generates your standardized job title and message preview based on your chosen service category.
2. In the optional **Instructions** field, enter specific details about your household expectations, house size (e.g., "2-bedroom bungalow"), and routine chores.
3. Include any relevant house rules or conditions (such as "Pet friendly", "Bring own specialized tools", or "Non-smoker preferred").
4. Tap **Next** to proceed to schedule and location details.
