# Start Registration & Select Homeowner Role

Begin your journey on SerbiSure by creating a dedicated Homeowner account.

1. Open the **SerbiSure** app on your phone.
2. On the welcome screen, tap the **Get Started** button.
3. On the role selection screen titled *"How would you like to join?"*, tap the **I am a Homeowner** card button.
4. Tapping this option automatically opens Step 1 of the Homeowner registration form.

> [!TIP]
> Choosing **I am a Homeowner** configures your account to post jobs, browse verified kasambahay profiles, and hire trusted household help.
