# Submit Your National ID

1. On **Step 4: Identity Documents**, select **National ID (Front)** and **National ID (Back)**.
2. Take or choose clear photos of both sides. Check that all details can be read.
3. Tap **Submit**. Your documents go to the reviewers.
4. You can use **Skip** to submit documents later from your profile. Posting and booking remain blocked until your required documents and face photo are approved.
