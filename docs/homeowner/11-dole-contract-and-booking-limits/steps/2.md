# Monthly Short-Term Limit on Chat Booking Offers

1. When sending a **Booking Offer** in chat to a worker who has already completed 3 short-term bookings with you this month, the app blocks additional short-term offers with **Cannot Send Offer**.
2. Tap **OK** to close the alert and return to the booking offer form.
3. Change the engagement type to **Long-term** so the system generates a **DOLE Standard Kasambahay Contract** PDF (which you can download from your confirmed booking details).
