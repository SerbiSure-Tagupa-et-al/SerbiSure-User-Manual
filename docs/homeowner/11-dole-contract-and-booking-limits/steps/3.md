# Meet the Minimum Monthly Salary for Long-Term Contracts

1. In **Booking Offer — Step 3 of 4 (Location)** for a **Long-term** engagement, enter the work address and scroll to **AGREED MONTHLY SALARY**.
2. If you enter an amount below the statutory threshold (for example, 700 / month), SerbiSure highlights the field in red and displays **Minimum: ₱6,500 / month**.
3. Enter at least **₱6,500 / month** and tap **Next** to review and send your compliant long-term contract offer.
