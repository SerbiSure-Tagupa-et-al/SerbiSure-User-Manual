# Browse Public Posts

1. Tap the briefcase icon in the bottom navigation bar to browse posts.
2. Use **Explore** for new posts or **Saved** for posts you bookmarked.
3. Read the service, location, work setup, rate and daily hours.

On **Home**, the **Recent Posts** cards have separate tap areas. Tap the worker's name or photo to open their profile. Tap the description to open full details. The swipe-deck controls stay the same.
