# Review Saved Candidates in the Saved Tab

1. Tap the **Saved** tab at the top of the **Find Kasambahay** screen to view all bookmarked or liked worker cards in one list.
2. Check each saved worker's service category, engagement type, and monthly or daily rate.
3. Tap the **Chat** icon to message the worker directly, tap **See Details** to reopen the full post, or tap the orange **Bookmark** ribbon to remove them from your saved list.
