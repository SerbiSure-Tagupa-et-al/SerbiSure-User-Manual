# Swipe Through Candidates in Explore

1. Tap the **Briefcase** icon in the bottom navigation bar to open **Find Kasambahay**.
2. Make sure the **Explore** tab is selected at the top of the screen, and use the quick filter pills (**Filters**, **All**, **Stay-in**, **Stay-out**) to narrow results.
3. Review the worker card summary, then tap the green **Heart** button (or bookmark icon) to save the candidate, tap the red **X** button to pass, or tap **See Details** to inspect their full service post.
