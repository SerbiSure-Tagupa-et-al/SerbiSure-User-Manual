# Inspect Role Details & Message Kasambahay

1. After tapping **See Details** on an Explore card, read the worker's rate, engagement badge (**Short-term** or **Long-term**), **Work hours**, and **ABOUT THIS ROLE** description.
2. Tap the **Bookmark** ribbon icon in the upper-right corner of the sheet if you want to save or unsave the post for later review.
3. Tap **Message Kasambahay** at the bottom of the sheet to open a direct chat thread with the worker.
