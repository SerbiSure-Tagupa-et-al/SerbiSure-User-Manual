# Follow the 6-Step Spotlight App Tutorial

1. When the **App Tutorial** starts, a spotlight ring highlights each core navigation button one step at a time (starting with **1/6: Find a Kasambahay**).
2. Read the short explanation card for the highlighted feature.
3. Tap **Next** to advance through all 6 spotlight steps, or tap the **X** icon in the corner of the card to exit the tutorial early.
