# Open Support & Legal (App Tutorial & User Manual)

1. In **Profile**, scroll down to the **SUPPORT & LEGAL** section below **PREFERENCES**.
2. Tap **App Tutorial** anytime to replay the interactive 6-step spotlight walkthrough of SerbiSure.
3. Tap **User Manual** directly below **App Tutorial** to launch the complete illustrated SerbiSure User Manual, or open **About Us**, **Privacy Policy**, and **Terms and Conditions**.
