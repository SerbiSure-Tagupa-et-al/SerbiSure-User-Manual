# Browse & Download the SerbiSure User Manual

1. After tapping **User Manual** in settings, select your role tab (**Homeowner**, **Kasambahay**, or **Admin / Barangay**) or filter by topic.
2. Tap **Download Word (.docx)** to save an offline Microsoft Word copy of the manual to your device.
3. Tap **Print / Save PDF** to print or export the complete manual as a PDF document.
