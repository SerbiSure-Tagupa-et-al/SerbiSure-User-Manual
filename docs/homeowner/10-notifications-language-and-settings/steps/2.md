# Switch Language (English, Tagalog, Bisaya) & Hide Phone Number

1. Open **Profile** from the bottom navigation bar and scroll to **PREFERENCES** (*MGA KAGUSTUHAN*).
2. Toggle **Show Contact Number** (*Ipakita ang Numero ng Telepono*) off to keep your phone number marked **Private** (*Pribado*) on your public profile.
3. Tap the **Language** (*Wika*) selector pill and choose **English**, **Tagalog**, or **Bisaya** to immediately translate the app interface.
