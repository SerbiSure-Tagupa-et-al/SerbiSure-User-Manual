# View Full Role Details & Start a Chat

1. Tap **See Details** on any card in the deck to open the detail sheet showing the pay rate, **Short-term** or **Long-term** badge, **Work hours**, and **ABOUT THIS ROLE**.
2. Tap the **Bookmark** ribbon icon in the upper-right corner of the sheet to bookmark the listing.
3. Tap the primary action button at the bottom of the sheet to open a direct conversation in **Messages**.
