# Manage Bookmarked Listings in the Saved Tab

1. Switch from **Explore** to **Saved** at the top of the screen to view all listings you have bookmarked.
2. Review the saved card's category, engagement badge, and offered rate.
3. Tap the **Chat** button to message directly, tap **See Details** to review the full description, or tap the orange **Bookmark** icon to remove it from **Saved**.
