# Browse Listings on the Explore Swipe Deck

1. Tap the **Briefcase** icon in the bottom navigation bar to open the **Explore** swipe deck.
2. Use **Explore** and the quick filter pills (**Filters**, **All**, **Stay-in**, **Stay-out**) to filter listings by work arrangement and rate.
3. Tap the green **Heart** button (or bookmark icon) to save a card to your **Saved** list, tap the red **X** button to skip, or tap **See Details** to view full requirements.
