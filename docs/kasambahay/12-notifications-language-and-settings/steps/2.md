# Change App Language (English, Tagalog, Bisaya) & Phone Privacy

1. Go to **Profile** and scroll to **PREFERENCES** (*MGA KAGUSTUHAN*).
2. Switch **Show Contact Number** (*Ipakita ang Numero ng Telepono*) off if you want your phone number hidden as **Private** (*Pribado*) on your profile.
3. Tap **Language** (*Wika*) and select **English**, **Tagalog**, or **Bisaya** to use SerbiSure in your preferred language.
