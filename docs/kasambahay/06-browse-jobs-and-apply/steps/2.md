# Filter the Posts

1. Open **Filters** to see **Filter & Sort**.
2. Choose **SERVICE CATEGORY**, **BOOKING TYPE** and **WORK SETUP** as needed.
3. Set a maximum daily or monthly rate if you want a price limit.
4. Enter an area under **LOCATION / BARANGAY**.
5. Close the panel to apply your filters and browse. Use **Reset all filters** to clear your choices.
