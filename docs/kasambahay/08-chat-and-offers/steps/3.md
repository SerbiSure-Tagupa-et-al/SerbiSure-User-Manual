# Accept the Booking Offer or Negotiate Terms

Finalize the work agreement.

1. If you are satisfied with the dates, hours, and pay, tap the green **Accept Offer** button.
2. If the dates do not work for you, tap **Decline** or send a message in chat suggesting an alternative time.
3. Once you tap **Accept**, the booking is locked in and officially moved to your **Confirmed Bookings** schedule!

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A booking offer whose start date has already passed cannot be accepted. If the date has passed, the app displays: *"Cannot accept: Original date has passed. Please send a new offer in chat with an updated date."* Arrange a fresh future date with the employer directly in the chat thread.
