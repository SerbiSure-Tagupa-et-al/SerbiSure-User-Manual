# Open the SerbiSure App to the Sign In Screen

Access your Kasambahay account login.

1. Open the **SerbiSure** app on your phone.
2. On the welcome screen, tap **Log in** at the bottom.
3. The sign-in form opens with input fields for your phone/email and password.

**Startup help:** On launch, SerbiSure checks for updates on a branded starting screen. If the check takes over 6 seconds or fails, check your internet and tap **Try Again**. An app update downloads and restarts SerbiSure. For an Android installer update, tap **Update Now** and follow your phone's **Update** or **Allow** prompt. Use **Finish Update** if prompted. A failed update check cannot be skipped.
