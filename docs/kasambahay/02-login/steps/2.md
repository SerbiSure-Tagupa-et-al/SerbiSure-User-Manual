# Enter Your Contact Number and Password

Type your registered credentials.

1. Tap the **Email or phone number** field and enter your 11-digit mobile number or registered email.
2. Tap the **Password** field and type your password.
3. Tap the **Eye Icon** to verify that you typed your password correctly.
4. Tap the dark **Log in** button.
5. You will land directly on the Kasambahay Dashboard!
