# Choose the Kasambahay Worker Role

Start your registration to offer household services on SerbiSure.

1. Launch the **SerbiSure** app on your mobile device.
2. Tap the **Get Started** button on the welcome screen.
3. On the role selection screen titled *"How would you like to join?"*, tap the **I am a Kasambahay** card button.
4. Tapping this option automatically opens Step 1 of the Kasambahay registration form.

> [!TIP]
> Choosing **I am a Kasambahay** sets up your account to complete worker verification, build your professional CV, apply for jobs, and offer household services.
