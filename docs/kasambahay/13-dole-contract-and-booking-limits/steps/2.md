# Transitioning from Short-Term to a Long-Term DOLE Contract

1. When the 3-booking monthly short-term limit is reached in chat, **Cannot Send Offer** reminds both parties to use a **Long-term** engagement.
2. Once a **Long-term** booking offer is accepted and confirmed, open your booking details in **My Jobs & Contracts** to tap **Download DOLE Standard Kasambahay Contract**.
3. Cancelling a confirmed booking without valid grounds may incur monthly penalty points (reaching 3/3 restricts new bookings).
