# Minimum Monthly Wage Protection (₱6,500 / Month)

1. For every **Long-term** booking offer, SerbiSure validates the **AGREED MONTHLY SALARY** in **Step 3 of 4 (Location)**.
2. Any monthly salary below the statutory floor (such as 700 / month) is blocked with the red notice **Minimum: ₱6,500 / month**.
3. The homeowner must set the monthly salary to at least **₱6,500 / month** before the offer can proceed to review.
