# Monthly Short-Term Limit When Applying or Confirming

1. To protect domestic workers under labor guidelines, short-term bookings are capped at **7 days** and a maximum of **3 short-term bookings per month** with the same homeowner.
2. If you and the same homeowner reach 3 short-term bookings in a month, attempting another short-term booking shows **Application not confirmed**.
3. Tap **OK** and ask the homeowner to send a **Long-term** booking offer covered by the **DOLE Standard Kasambahay Contract**.
