# Edit Services, Rates and Experience

1. In **Edit CV Details**, choose **SERVICES YOU OFFER** and **WORK YOU ARE LOOKING FOR**.
2. Enter **EXPECTED MONTHLY SALARY** and **EXPECTED DAILY RATE**.
3. Select **SPOKEN LANGUAGES** and **HOUSEHOLD WORK BEFORE**.
4. Choose your **APPROXIMATE EXPERIENCE**. The optional skills selector below it also uses **APPROXIMATE EXPERIENCE** as its label; choose the practical skills you have.
