# Review & Publish Your Public Service Listing

Make your service visible to homeowners in your area.

1. Review the generated **Listing Preview** and **Summary** card with your service category, rate, location, and availability schedule.
2. Tap the checkbox to review and agree to the SerbiSure **Terms & Conditions**.
3. Tap the primary **Post Listing** button.
4. Your service listing is now live! Homeowners browsing for help in your barangay can now discover your service and send you direct booking requests.
