# Review and Post Your Service

1. Read **LISTING PREVIEW** and **SUMMARY**. Check the service, engagement, work setup, rate, location and **Work hours**.
2. Tap the agreement checkbox. Read and accept the terms that open.
3. Tap **Post Service**.
4. Wait for **Service posted!** before leaving the form.

To remove your service later, open **Profile**, then **Personal Info** and **My Recent Posts**. Open the post, tap **Cancel Post**, then confirm **Cancel Post**.
