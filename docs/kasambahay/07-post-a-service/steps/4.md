# Add "About You" Notes & Highlight Your Strengths

Describe what makes your household service dependable and thorough.

1. SerbiSure automatically formats your public listing title and summary based on your chosen service category.
2. In the optional **About You** field, add personal notes about your work style, attention to detail, and specific household strengths.
3. Highlight any special certifications or practical skills (such as laundry care, infant care, or elderly assistance).
4. Tap **Next** to proceed to the review screen.
