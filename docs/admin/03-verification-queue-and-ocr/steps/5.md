# Correct Confirmed Details

1. Click **Edit details** in the document review panel.
2. Correct only details you can confirm from the document.
3. Check **Account details** and **Document details**, including names, birthdate, document number and clearance dates where shown.
4. Click **Save corrections**.

The change is recorded in the Audit Trail. Saving corrections does not approve the document. Login, contact information, role and barangay cannot be changed here.
