# Update the Desk Profile or Password

1. In your barangay settings, update **Desk Officer in Charge**, **Official Desk Hotline**, **Physical Desk / Barangay Hall Location** and **Office Operating Hours & Public Notice** as needed.
2. Click **Save Desk Profile** and wait for confirmation.
3. To change your password, enter the current password and the new password twice, then click **Update Password**.

These settings do not change QR pass validity or gate-entry rules.
