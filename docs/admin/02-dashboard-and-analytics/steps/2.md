# Read Verification Status and Employment Trends

1. Read the verification status chart for **Verified**, **Pending**, **Unverified**, **Expired** and **Rejected** accounts.
2. Use its role and barangay filters where your account allows them.
3. Read the employment trend for **Occupied** and **Available** workers.
4. A month with **No history** has no recorded history; do not treat it as zero workers.
